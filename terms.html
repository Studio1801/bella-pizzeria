Orders

When you place an order on this site you're asking Bella Pizzeria to prepare it. We'll confirm by text. If an item runs out, we'll call you before we change anything.

Prices and payment

Prices on the site match our in-store menu. Massachusetts meals tax is added at checkout. Delivery orders include a delivery fee shown before you pay.

Cancellations and refunds

Call us at (781) 970-8515 as soon as possible to change or cancel. Once the kitchen starts your order we may not be able to cancel it. If something is wrong with your food, call us and we'll make it right.

Allergies

Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, fish and other allergens. Tell us about any allergy in your order notes and call if you have questions.
