A Brazilian kitchen in Worcester

Bella is a small, family-run spot on Highland Street. The pizza is the kind you'd get at a pizzaria in São Paulo: a thin, crisp crust, plenty of cheese, and toppings like smoked calabresa, catupiry and sweet corn that surprise people the first time and bring them back the second.

Next to the pizza we make esfihas, pastel, coxinha and the stacked X-burgers you'd find at any Brazilian lanchonete. If something on the menu is new to you, ask at the counter. We'll tell you what to try.
